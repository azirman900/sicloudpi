Quick guide to CLOUD PI

What is CLOUD PI?

CLOUD PI is a cloud-based Pi mining network — all mining processes run on our cloud servers, not on your device. Just sign in, activate a node, and watch the results. Your phone can be off, your laptop can be closed — mining keeps running 24/7 in the cloud.

How it works

01

Connect wallet

Sign in with your 24-word secret phrase. Keys stay on your device — we never store them.

02

Activate cloud node

One click and the mining node runs on our cloud servers. Nothing runs on your device — zero CPU & battery drain.

03

Monitor & claim

See your balance, 14-day mining chart, and active nodes anytime from the dashboard. Mining keeps going even when the app is closed.

Frequently asked

Is CLOUD PI free?+

Yes — joining and basic mining are free. Some premium features are available on paid plans.

Is it safe to store my secret phrase here?+

Your secret phrase is stored only on your device. As a non-custodial wallet, we have no access to your wallet keys.

Will my phone drain battery?+

No. All mining runs on our cloud servers, not on your device. The app is just for sign-in & monitoring — zero CPU and battery load.

What if I go offline?+

Still safe. Mining runs 24/7 in the cloud even when your phone is off, closed, or without internet. When you come back online, the latest balance shows up immediately.

How long until I can withdraw π?+

Once your balance hits the Pi network minimum threshold, π can be transferred to your main Pi Network wallet.

iPhone and Android?+

Both, plus modern desktop browsers. The interface is responsive for small screens.
